# Layered paper characters and dungeon biomes

The original direction sheet is `images/paper-biomes-concept.png`; the latest roster revision is `images/paper-biomes-concept-v3.png` (v2 is retained). Exact historical prompts are retained in `paper-biomes-prompt.txt`, `paper-biomes-prompt-v2.txt` and `paper-biomes-prompt-v3.txt`. It is an aspirational concept, not a screenshot of this renderer. Environmental references: [Frost World](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.winter.breaker), [Train of Hope](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.samfinaco.tos); paper craft reference: [Papetura](https://petums.com/papetura/). No reference game assets, logos or textures were copied.

`dungeon_biomes.gd.for_floor(int)` derives foundry, frost, overgrown_rail in a repeating three-floor cycle, with floor 1 remaining Foundry. The existing floor number remains the sole saved input. `FloorGenerator` passes the same region to environment art, landmarks and guard art. Optional biome arguments default to Foundry for existing callers. Foundry and the existing paper town retain their environment path.

Frozen Frontier adds pale slab/grout and frosted masonry palettes, snow-colored slate caps, wall snow ledges, turquoise ice panels, frosted pipes and buried heater cabinets. Overgrown Rails adds shallow rails/sleepers, moss palettes, wall station/engine remnants and thick cut-paper foliage outside walkable footprints. Geometry is baked at generation, with no new lights, physics, navigation, billboard or transparency systems. Existing caps remain at or below 2.72 and grout stays at 0.040 between the 0.036 source accents and 0.0475 slab tops.

`paper_geometry.gd` caches five closed folded-card unit forms with offset thin face layers and raised seams, matte vertex-color materials and actual depth. All three heroes share these volumes and the existing pose/node paths. The Infiltrator uses blond hair, green tunic and folded pointed cap, a straight steel Trail Sword with blue-purple winged guard/wrapped grip and an original teal leaf shield secured diagonally to the back with visible leather straps. The left hand stays empty. The shield is decoration assigned to the body pose role, with no block, collider, durability, damage or input mechanic. Shadowblade retains silver hair/dark coat with a thick clipped steel Buster Sword; Riftwarden retains ivory mask, dark hood and crimson cape, now with two separated red energy prongs in place of the visible chain. The legacy rift_chain resource ID and ChainBlade node path remain valid. Opaque emission adds no lights, transparency or shader.

`trail_sword.tres` copies the old starter's attack, durability, degradation, break policy and build-up branch. It is the new default starter and is explicitly save-whitelisted. `battle_wrench` remains unchanged and whitelisted; existing saves are not migrated or stripped. Existing actors and weapon hitboxes still own combat timing and ranges.

`enemy_art.gd` caches a small set of non-gory paper-machine sentries: cream ribs, matte brass piston joints, dark cables, amber core and an asymmetric arm. Training actors keep their stationary behavior; guards keep their existing club MeshInstance3D and attack timeline. Body remains a MeshInstance3D with an independently owned StandardMaterial3D for hit flashing. No enemy AI or collision changes are intended.

## Verification

Execution was initially deferred, then explicitly authorized. Native regression checks cover closed signed-volume/winding geometry, cache/depth budgets, new and legacy saved inventories, back-shield ownership, independent enemy flashes, biome layout stability, imported palette/nested bounds and landmark clearance. Native/Android captures confirm the functional identities, back shield and separated red prongs. The meshes remain deliberately simple prototypes and do not match the concept's detail density. Exact local/Android results are in outputs/Validation-results.txt; physical-phone performance is unmeasured.

Future work should broaden generated seeds, inspect more combat poses and paper edges, and measure generation/node/triangle budgets and actual phone frame behavior. Existing legacy Battle Wrench save coverage remains alongside Trail Sword, renamed roster and energy-prong checks.

Camera work is separate: the requested yaw escape was implemented after the art source in adventure_camera.gd and the persistence interface; see CORNER_CAMERA.md. Both are covered by the current native/Android validation. Do not infer that new art fixes camera obstruction.
