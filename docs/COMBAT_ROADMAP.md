# Future ranged combat

Current heroes share the existing melee controller. Planned ranged identities are Infiltrator: boomerang; Shadowblade: throwing stars; Riftwarden: laser whip; Battlemage: wand. These are deferred design directions, with no ranged attacks, controls, projectiles, or balance changes implemented.

Use the user's mechanics list as brainstorming input: variable rewards, nostalgia, flow/social comparison, reward systems/types, agency, escape and relaxation. Favor agency, skill, fairness and meaningful progression over pressure or repetitive grind. User-supplied research citations remain unverified. No online leaderboard is planned in this scope.

Approved future weaponless core upgrade plan: limited jaw-force tiers for boss damage, armor tiers for HP/defense, and efficiency tiers for recovery, with town respec. This is a plan only; no hidden weapon or new currency grind is implemented. Devourer unlocks after clearing floor6; upgrades remain unimplemented.
